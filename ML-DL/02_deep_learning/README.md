# Part 2: Deep Learning

Deep learning models and how they are trained. The material is based on Bishop & Bishop (2024) and on Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville (2016). Full references are in the [course README](../README.md).

| Notebook | Overview | Bishop & Bishop (2024) |
|---|---|---|
| [`04_regression.ipynb`](04_regression.ipynb) | Single-layer networks for regression: least squares and maximum likelihood, basis functions, overfitting and weight decay, and the bias–variance trade-off. | Ch. 4 |
| [`05_classification.ipynb`](05_classification.ipynb) | Single-layer networks for classification: linear discriminants, the perceptron, decision theory, and logistic and softmax regression. | Ch. 5 |
| [`06_deep_networks.ipynb`](06_deep_networks.ipynb) | Multilayer networks: why fixed basis functions fall short, universal approximation, representation learning, and error functions for deep models. | Ch. 6 |
| [`07_gradient_descent.ipynb`](07_gradient_descent.ipynb) | Training with gradient descent: error-surface geometry, stochastic and mini-batch updates, momentum, and adaptive methods such as Adam. | Ch. 7 |
| [`08_backpropagation.ipynb`](08_backpropagation.ipynb) | Derives backpropagation on computational graphs and explains why reverse-mode differentiation is efficient. | Ch. 8 |
| [`09_regularization.ipynb`](09_regularization.ipynb) | Controls model complexity with weight decay, $L_1$ sparsity, early stopping, dropout and batch normalization. | Ch. 9 |
| [`10_convolutional_networks.ipynb`](10_convolutional_networks.ipynb) | Convolutional networks: locality and translation equivariance, convolution and pooling, and the path from LeNet-5 to ResNet. | Ch. 10 |
| [`11_sequence_models.ipynb`](11_sequence_models.ipynb) | Recurrent networks, backpropagation through time, LSTMs and sequence-to-sequence models, leading to the first attention mechanisms. | supplementary |
| [`12_transformers.ipynb`](12_transformers.ipynb) | Transformers: scaled dot-product and multi-head attention, positional encodings, and the full transformer architecture. | Ch. 12 |
| [`13_graph_neural_networks.ipynb`](13_graph_neural_networks.ipynb) | Graph neural networks: permutation symmetries, message passing, and graph convolutional and attention networks. | Ch. 13 |
